# Checked, and left off this clock

The question for this plan is one sender, the family curve, both machines publishing. Anything below would change that measurement or does not yet have a public covenant to count. Pulse can name a day for any of them on its own.

## An app under a load that is already stable

**1984 water.** [tn10-1984-water-2026-10-17](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-1984-water-2026-10-17) is frozen after the 16 Oct agreement with Pulse. The 17 Oct plan was a modest run, about 600 tx/s, with the cafe buying one water while senders were already stable, and a live fee with a hard cap. The congestion ramp was written as a future run. The worked write-up of the capped fee is AUTO-FEE R0 in [tn10-storm-plan-mint-rush](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-plan-mint-rush). The site lives on [sixpack.wtf](https://github.com/STP-KAS/sixpack.wtf). It is not a product and not Kaspa core. A fee-capped app during a later load is its own night, so the family confirmation stays one question. That is the test of how a small app fares while the network is busy. [tn-master ADVICE](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn-master/blob/main/ADVICE-TO-DEVELOPERS.md) item 1 is the reason.

**Mint rush.** [tn10-storm-plan-mint-rush](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn10-storm-plan-mint-rush) is already its own plan. No date. Auto-fee against a fixed fee, under storm load. [Open question 8](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn-master/blob/main/docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md). It is not folded into this clock.

## Covenants that are not ready for a TN10 count

**Kaspa Dollar.** In [kaspa-builders](https://github.com/STP-KAS/kaspa-builders) it is still a claim. The catalog check found no code and no deployment, and it was not desk-tested. A public page is not a covenant this plan can submit. It stays a study until there is a public covenant to count.

**Kaspa Vault Lab.** [kas-vault-lab](https://github.com/STP-KAS/kas-vault-lab) is a study. The public TN10 broadcast did not land. Later scripts ran on a private localhost devnet. The quote is a lab key. Liquidation entries are stored and not spent. That is a different question from the family curve. It is not this clock.

**Name services, bridges, privacy experiments, and other catalog rows** in kaspa-builders are labeled catalog or claim. A catalog row is not a measured TN10 count. A builder reading that repo still needs the source on the row. This plan does not promote any of them into a load.

## Already measured, or not a load

**Many senders, simple payments.** 10 Oct and 11 Oct already counted that shape. The 11 Oct peak complete minute was 3,583.1 tx/s and the mean was 2,833.1. The open piece is whether the network can hold that peak for an hour ([open question 4](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn-master/blob/main/docs/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md)). Putting those senders on the family clock would leave the one-sender band. It is its own night if someone wants it again.

**vprog tic-tac-toe.** On 25 Sep, n0 carried about 17 hours of load while vprogs and the vprog tic-tac-toe were also running ([history](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn-master/blob/main/history/MASTER.md)). [blue-line-tictactoe](https://github.com/STP-KAS/blue-line-tictactoe) is a local referee, one blue mark per ply. It does not submit to testnet. Neither one is this covenant curve.

**Tic-tac-toe fee through a proxy, under storm.** [Advice item 4](https://github.com/STP-KAS/tn-master/blob/main/ADVICE-TO-DEVELOPERS.md): the carriers paid the floor. Raising the fee estimate through a proxy changed nothing, and 0 games finished in 12 minutes of storm. That lesson is already written. A later night can ask whether the fee an app sets is the fee the node uses, on a load that is already stable. It is not this family clock.

**pow-rail.** [pow-rail](https://github.com/STP-KAS/pow-rail) is a notes repo about a public proof-of-work rail. It is not a storm kit and it does not send.

**kaspa-master-file.** [kaspa-master-file](https://github.com/STP-KAS/kaspa-master-file) is a sourced pin board. A pin is not consensus law, and the board is not a load.

**Governance.** The desk repos do not contain an on-chain governance program to put under congestion. KIP-17 names congestion control as one use of covenants. That is a document. It is not a test this desk has run.

**Freeport.** A search of the STP-KAS repos found no project under that name. This plan does not invent one.
